# The Berkeley Major Arbitrage Engine

Question: Among fall-2025 Berkeley transfer applicants, how much does admit rate
vary across majors that teach substantially overlapping skills?

 Time window: fall 2025
- Population: Berkeley transfer applicants
- Metric: admit rate (admits / applicants), with admit-GPA percentiles

Finding: Near-identical skillsets are priced wildly differently. Computer Science
admits 3% of transfer applicants; Applied Math admits 38% for overlapping coursework,
and the admit-GPA bar barely moves across the whole cluster (3.8-4.0). The gap is
competition volume, not student caliber, so choosing the less-crowded major name is a
real admissions arbitrage.

Gemini* A student describes their goal in plain English; Gemini maps it to the
cluster of skill-adjacent majors, then writes a strategy grounded only in the real
admit rates and GPA percentiles shown on screen.
